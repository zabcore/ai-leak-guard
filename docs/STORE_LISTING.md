## Short description (≤ 132 characters)

## Detailed description
AI Leak Guard is a privacy layer between healthcare workers and the AI tools they already use every day.

When you paste text into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot, AI Leak Guard checks it first — right in your browser — for the kinds of details that identify a patient: names, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, medical record and member numbers, and government or financial identifiers. If it finds any, it shows you exactly what it would hide and lets you decide: paste the protected version, paste the original as-is, or cancel. Nothing is sent until you choose.

Clinical details on their own — a medication name or a diagnosis code — are treated as context, not as something to hide, so you only see a prompt when there is a real identifier to protect. No noise on ordinary text.

How it works
- Detection happens entirely on your device. Your text is never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.
- You see a preview of what will be hidden before anything is inserted, and you make the final call.
- It works on the major AI chat tools and stays out of your way the rest of the time.

Built to be trustworthy
- 100% local. No account, no login, no backend, no analytics.
- Free for individual healthcare workers.
- Open source, so anyone can read exactly what it does.

What AI Leak Guard does
- Spots common patient identifiers before you paste them into an AI tool.
- Lets you replace those details with placeholders in one click.
- Helps your team follow an acceptable-use policy for AI.

What AI Leak Guard does not do
- It does not make ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot compliant with any regulation.
- It does not guarantee that every possible identifier is caught.
- It is not a substitute for your organization's privacy and security program, and it is not legal or compliance advice.

AI Leak Guard reduces the chance and the impact of accidentally sharing patient information with an AI tool. It is a preventive privacy control that keeps you in control.

## Permission justifications
- Host access to the supported AI sites: required to read the text you paste into those sites' chat boxes so it can be checked locally before insertion. The content is processed in the page and never leaves the browser.
- Storage: used only to remember whether the extension is enabled and a local count of items masked. No text you type or paste is stored.

## Privacy practices
- This item does not collect or transmit user data.
- All processing is local to the user's browser.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.
